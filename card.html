Things you need to know

Where does my photo go?

Nowhere. The card is put together inside your browser on your own phone or computer, so the photo never reaches a server. Close the page and it is gone.

My face is cut off

Drag the photo inside the circle to move it, and use the zoom slider to pull it closer or further back. The card redraws as you move.

Nothing happens when I press download

On some phones the card opens in a new tab instead of saving. Press and hold it, then choose save to your photos. The share button is usually the easier route on a phone.

Can I make one for someone else?

Yes. Press start over, put in their name and photo, and make as many as you need.
